# EthernaCare Updated Use Case Content

## Review of the Existing Diagram

The existing diagram contains the main ideas of check-in, status update, emergency alerts, location, notifications, rewards, Legacy Planning, and AI assistance. However, several implemented functions and external actors are missing or combined too broadly.

The actor labels are also cut off in the exported PNG because the actors were placed outside the visible canvas. The supplied PNG is a flattened image and cannot be edited as a draw.io diagram without the original `.drawio` source file.

### Missing or Inaccurate Elements

1. **Authentication and account management** are missing, including registration, email verification, login, password recovery, Google login, and optional biometric unlock.
2. **Profile and safety settings** are missing, including the inactivity threshold and validated home address.
3. **Trusted contact management and phone verification** are missing.
4. **Automatic inactivity monitoring** is not clearly separated from a manually triggered SOS alert.
5. **Legacy Planning and Legacy Checking** are combined even though they are performed by different actors and use different access rules.
6. **Secure Legacy Notes and document management** are missing.
7. **Reward collection, voucher codes, and administrator catalogue management** are missing.
8. **Hospital** is not the correct actor. The application may open the Malaysian emergency-services dialler (`999`), but it does not directly dispatch a hospital.
9. **Family & Trusted Contact** is too broad. Safety and Legacy access are restricted to the user's verified primary trusted contact.
10. **Family Notifying** should be renamed to **Notify Primary Trusted Contact**.
11. **Location Tracking** should not imply continuous tracking. Current device location is requested for a manual SOS when permission is available; the profile home address is stored separately.
12. **Virtual Pet Check-In** should distinguish a safety check-in from ordinary Oren interactions such as feeding, playing, shopping, and selecting an owned item.
13. **Notification extends User Status Update** is ambiguous. Notifications are generated by reminder, emergency, verification, and Legacy workflows instead.

## Use Case Actors

### Primary User

Represents an elderly individual or an independent person living alone who uses EthernaCare.

The Primary User can register and verify an account, manage a profile and safety settings, add trusted contacts, verify phone numbers, perform safety check-ins through Oren, interact with Oren, earn and collect virtual rewards, trigger an SOS alert, create Legacy Planning information, upload secure documents, and use the AI assistance chatbot.

### Primary Trusted Contact

Represents the phone-verified contact selected by the Primary User as the primary contact.

The Primary Trusted Contact receives applicable inactivity and SOS notifications. During a manual SOS, the message may include the user's current location when location permission and positioning are available. The contact may also perform Legacy Checking using the user's Legacy UID, registered primary-contact phone number, and verification code.

After successful verification, funeral preferences may be viewed at any time. Protected Legacy Notes and secure documents are available only when the server-side inactivity and release conditions have been satisfied and the limited access window remains active.

### Administrator

Represents an authorised operator who uses a separate administrator interface.

The Administrator manages the virtual reward catalogue by creating, updating, refreshing, disabling, or deleting badge and voucher rewards. The Administrator does not receive access to a user's private Legacy Notes or documents.

### Emergency Services (999)

Represents the Malaysian emergency service that a user may choose to call during an emergency.

EthernaCare may open the device telephone dialler with `999`, but the user must initiate the call. The application does not directly contact or dispatch a hospital.

### External AI Service

Provides the artificial-intelligence service used by the AI assistance chatbot to process user questions and generate guidance.

The chatbot provides general assistance and is not a replacement for emergency, medical, legal, or professional advice.

### Messaging and Email Services

Represent external delivery services used by the backend for phone verification codes, safety messages, Legacy verification, account emails, and Legacy release notifications.

These services deliver messages requested by EthernaCare's server-side functions. They do not decide whether a user is inactive or whether Legacy access is authorised.

### External Identity, Location, and Weather Services

Represent supporting services used for Google authentication, address validation or geocoding, current-location retrieval, and weather information. These actors may be omitted from a simplified high-level diagram but should be included in a complete system use-case model.

## Main Use Cases

### Account Registration and Authentication

Allows the Primary User to create an account, verify an email address, sign in, recover or change a password, use Google authentication, and optionally enable biometric unlocking on a supported device.

Biometrics protect access to an existing authenticated session; they do not replace the Supabase account or server-side authentication.

### Profile and Safety Settings

Allows the Primary User to update personal information, validate and save a home address, select an inactivity threshold, configure notifications, and manage security or accessibility preferences.

### Trusted Contact Management

Allows the Primary User to add, edit, remove, and phone-verify trusted contacts. One verified contact can be selected as the primary trusted contact for safety escalation and Legacy Checking.

### Oren Interaction and Safety Check-In

Allows the user to interact with Oren through petting, feeding, playing, shopping, and selecting owned items.

When a safety check-in is due, the check-in action confirms activity, records the check-in on the server, resets the current inactivity-reminder cycle, and updates the user's safety status. Ordinary Oren care actions should not be described as safety check-ins unless they explicitly complete the due check-in.

### Automatic Inactivity Monitoring

Runs on the backend according to the user's configured inactivity threshold. The worker evaluates the latest heartbeat or check-in, updates the current status, records missed check-in periods, sends the configured user reminders, and escalates to the verified primary trusted contact when the escalation conditions are met.

A successful check-in starts a new monitoring cycle. This workflow does not depend on the mobile application remaining open in the background.

### Manual SOS Emergency Alert

Allows the Primary User to deliberately trigger an emergency alert. The system records the SOS event, attempts to retrieve the device's current location, and asks the backend to notify the verified primary trusted contact.

If current location is unavailable, the alert should still be sent with a clear location-unavailable message or an appropriate stored home-address fallback. Opening the `999` dialler is an optional extension of the SOS workflow.

### Location Handling

Validates the user's saved home address and attempts to retrieve current device coordinates for a manual SOS when permission is granted.

EthernaCare does not continuously track the user. Location access is associated with the emergency workflow and depends on device permission, positioning availability, and connectivity.

### Virtual Reward Management

Rewards consistent check-in behaviour with virtual badges or vouchers. An earned reward can be collected into the user's Reward Collection, and a voucher may include a generated redemption code.

Reward unlocking is an optional result of a qualifying check-in or milestone. Administrator catalogue management is a separate use case from user reward collection.

### Legacy Planning

Allows the Primary User to record funeral and service preferences, select an authorised contact from existing trusted contacts, create protected Legacy Notes, and upload supported secure documents.

Users are warned not to store passwords, authentication codes, or account credentials in Legacy Notes.

### Legacy Checking

Allows the verified primary trusted contact to request access using the user's Legacy UID, the registered primary-contact phone number, and a verification code.

Funeral preferences can be viewed after successful contact verification. Protected Legacy Notes and secure documents remain locked until the required inactivity period, final user-protection process, and release conditions are satisfied. Once released, protected access is available only during the configured seven-day window.

### Notification Delivery

Provides local notifications and server-originated SMS or email messages for account verification, phone verification, inactivity reminders, emergency alerts, and Legacy access events.

Notification Delivery supports other use cases and should not be modelled as an extension of User Status Update alone.

### AI Assistance Chatbot

Allows users to ask questions and receive general guidance about EthernaCare, well-being, elderly care, emergency preparation, and related information. User queries are processed through the External AI Service.

## Recommended Diagram Structure

For a readable final diagram, place these actors outside the EthernaCare system boundary:

- Primary User on the left.
- Primary Trusted Contact on the right.
- Administrator on the right.
- Emergency Services (999) on the right.
- External AI Service on the right.
- Messaging and Email Services on the right.
- Optional external identity, location, and weather services on the right or in a second technical diagram.

Inside the system boundary, use separate ovals for:

- Register and Authenticate
- Manage Profile and Safety Settings
- Manage Trusted Contacts
- Verify Contact Phone
- Interact with Oren
- Perform Safety Check-In
- Update Safety Status
- Monitor Inactivity

- Capture Current Location

- Earn and Collect Virtual Rewards
- Manage Reward Catalogue

- Verify Legacy Contact
- View Funeral Preferences
